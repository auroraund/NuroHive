Adobe Illustrator / 2018

2D Rendering: Gannet

An exploratory 2D graphic rendering created using Adobe Illustrator. Combining vector paths and subtle gradient overlays, this piece pursues a sense of dimensionality and rich tactile graphic texture within a 2D medium.

Key Highlights & Approach

Texture & Depth in Illustrator — Carefully layering gradients and transparency values to reproduce realistic light reflections and product forms.
Vector Precision — Leveraging resolution-independent vector paths while achieving a high-fidelity, realistic visual quality.
