Bird Attracting Action Camera: Quaira

A proof-of-concept bird action camera fusing sound and photography into a single experience. The camera unit functions as a bird call connected to an egg-shaped handle via a cord. Swinging it in forests or woods attracts wild birds while capturing unique perspective footage.

Design & Concept Highlights

Bird-Call Camera Unit — Generates natural bird call sounds through air resistance when spun, attracting birds by lowering their alertness.
Egg-Shaped Handle & Cord System — An ergonomic egg-shaped grip connected via cord allows safe, intuitive centrifugal swinging of the camera.
3D Conceptual Design — Reimagines wildlife photography beyond conventional utility, visualizing a unique interactive experience through 3D CG product design.
