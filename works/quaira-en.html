Quaira

Bird Action Camera / Concept

About

Quaira is a concept action camera designed for birdwatching, combining auditory attraction with image capture. Connected by a cord to an egg-shaped handle, the camera unit incorporates a bird call structure that generates sound when rotated through the air to attract wild birds while capturing footage.

Tools & Environment

Design & 3D

Illustrator / Photoshop / Autodesk Fusion

Features

The camera unit itself generates natural bird-like sounds via wind resistance during rotation, reducing bird alertness. The ergonomic egg-shaped grip and durable cord facilitate smooth swinging movement, introducing an integrated approach to sound-based bird photography.
