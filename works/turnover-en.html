Transformable Hair Dryer: Turnover

A hair dryer that redefines "how to present non-use time." By incorporating a transformable body, it challenges the traditional concept of hiding appliances in drawers, standing as an aesthetic object that naturally blends into interior spaces when not in use.

Design & Feature Highlights

Interior-Friendly Transformation — Transforms into a compact form after use, leaving a refined silhouette that never adds visual noise to your room.
Rubber Cord Covering — Features a rubber component that neatly fits over the power cord, smartly concealing cluttered cables that typically spoil interior aesthetics.
