Sports water bottle: Gannet

Gannet is a specialized sports water bottle designed to support athletic recovery and hydration in one package.

Key features and benefits

Detachable icing sheet — Comes with a removable cooling sheet built directly into the bottle.
Post-workout support — Wrap the sheet around arms or legs after exercise to soothe and recover fatigued muscles.
